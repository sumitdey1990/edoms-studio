Privacy Policy.

Your privacy and data security are fundamental to our relationship. Here is how Edoms Studio handles and protects your data.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect essential information required to communicate with you and execute your design and engineering project, such as your name, business email, company details, and project requirements submitted via email or discovery forms.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to deliver our studio services, manage contracts, process invoices, and provide project updates. We will never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security & Storage

We employ enterprise-grade encryption and secure access controls to ensure your project files, assets, and correspondence are protected against unauthorized access.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, update, or permanently delete any personal information held by Edoms Studio by contacting privacy@edoms.studio.

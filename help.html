How Can We Help?

Find answers to common questions regarding our collaboration models, project timelines, deliverables, and support.

✦ What is the typical project timeline?

Most design & build projects take between 4 to 12 weeks depending on scope, complexity, and custom 3D/motion requirements. We provide a detailed milestone roadmap before kickoff.

✦ How do we communicate and share feedback?

We use dedicated Slack channels, weekly async video updates, and Figma comments for design iteration, ensuring transparent, real-time collaboration.

✦ Do you offer post-launch support and maintenance?

Yes. Every project includes a 30-day post-launch warranty, and we offer monthly retainer packages for continuous feature rollouts and optimization.

✦ How do we get started with a new project?

Simply reach out via email at hello@edoms.studio or request a proposal. We will schedule a discovery call within 24 hours.

Still have questions?

Our team is always ready to answer any questions or discuss custom project arrangements.
